    Snacks that fit

    Two a day, about 300 to 370 kcal in total. Each plan day pairs a sweet snack with a protein one.

      Snack rules

      • On Sunday, split the honey nuts into 30 g bags or boxes. Never eat from the big bag.
      • Pair a sweet snack with a protein snack. Protein keeps you full for longer.
      • Eat snacks from a bowl, sitting down.
      • Craving something in the evening? Drink sparkling water or a diet soda first and wait 10 minutes.
      • Rest days work the same way. Keep the portions.
